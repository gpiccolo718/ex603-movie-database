# Unit 4 Finding: The True Failure Rate

**To the leadership team**

The true failure rate on the platform is 17%. Of 200 ratings, 34 were withdrawn. Another 10 ratings (5%) are flagged for review but have not failed, so even if all of them were later withdrawn, the rate would be 22%. Either way, it is nowhere near the "about half" shown on the original dashboard.

The rate varies a lot between movies, from 0% for movie 10 (none of its 19 ratings withdrawn) up to 33% for movie 4. Movie 4's figure is misleading, though, because it has only 6 ratings, so a single withdrawal moves its rate by about 17 points. To compare fairly, I looked only at the eight movies with at least 15 ratings. Their typical withdrawal rate is about 16%, ranging from 0% to 31%. Movies 14 and 15 have no ratings at all and are not included.

I would investigate three movies:

- **Movie 11:** 5 of 16 ratings withdrawn (31%), nearly double the typical rate. It has the highest rate among the movies with enough ratings to trust.
- **Movie 2:** 4 of 16 ratings withdrawn (25%). Three of those four were removed as spam, which suggests this movie may be a target for fake ratings rather than having a quality problem.
- **Movie 9:** 4 of 14 ratings withdrawn (29%). It falls just short of the 15-rating cutoff, so its rate is less certain, but it is close enough and high enough to watch.
